The same rules on 18 markets

Before you trade it

  • On gold and Brent alone the improved version takes only about 4 trades a year. Across all 18 markets it takes about 65 a year, which gives a more reliable read: +0.15R a trade, or +0.12R on the 16 markets the rules were not tuned on.
  • Combined drawdown reached about 100R. At 1% risk per trade that is far too deep, so size each trade at around 0.25%.
  • Gold has been in a long bull market. Buying any random 2D open and holding for 5 bars made about +0.39R a trade, close to what the rejection longs made.
  • The results change a lot depending on which day 2D bars start on. TradingView's GC1! and UKOIL data will also differ from Yahoo's.
  • Forward-test the improved version, or add more markets, before trading it with real money.
