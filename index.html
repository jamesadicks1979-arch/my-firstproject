Evidence-Based Notes (Football Nutrition)

  • UEFA Expert Group statement: nutrition timing and fuel strategies in elite football.
  • Carbohydrate availability before training/match supports repeated high-intensity performance.
  • Post-session carbs + protein in the first recovery window supports glycogen restoration and muscle repair.
  • Hydration and electrolytes are crucial, especially around intense sessions and match day.

Sources: UEFA Expert Group Statement, JISSN Recovery Strategies in Soccer.
