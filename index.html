Front Page: Why the Right Fuel Timing Matters

Football performance is not just about what kids eat, but when they eat it. If a large meal is still being digested at kick-off, the body is working hard in the gut instead of fully supporting top running, focus, and power output.

1) Big meal window

Allow around 3-4 hours before kick-off or training.

A heavy dinner can take roughly 4 hours to digest well.

2) Final top-up

Small snack 60-90 minutes before (banana, toast, yogurt).

Keeps energy stable without a heavy stomach.

3) Recovery window

Carbs + protein in 30-60 minutes after training/match.

Supports glycogen refill, muscle repair, and next-session readiness.

Parent example: If Sunday match is 9:30 AM, make Saturday evening the main fueling meal, then keep Sunday breakfast light and easy to digest.

Evidence-Based Notes (Football Nutrition)

  • UEFA Expert Group statement: nutrition timing and fuel strategies in elite football.
  • Carbohydrate availability before training/match supports repeated high-intensity performance.
  • Post-session carbs + protein in the first recovery window supports glycogen restoration and muscle repair.
  • Hydration and electrolytes are crucial, especially around intense sessions and match day.

Sources: UEFA Expert Group Statement, JISSN Recovery Strategies in Soccer.
