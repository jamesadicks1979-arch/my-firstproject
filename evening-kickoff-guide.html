Simple Rule

Keep normal breakfast/lunch, then have the main pre-match meal around 3-4 hours before kick-off, and a small top-up snack 60-90 minutes before.

Heavy food too close to kick-off can increase stomach discomfort and reduce sharpness.

Post-Match Recovery (Important)

  • Within 30-60 mins: smoothie/milk + sandwich/wrap.
  • Rehydrate with water, add electrolytes if very sweaty.
  • Then eat a balanced dinner if appetite allows.

Recovery feeding supports muscle repair, refuels glycogen, and improves next-day readiness.
