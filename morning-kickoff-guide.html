Why not to eat loads in the morning

A big meal can take around 3-4 hours to digest. If your child eats too much close to kick-off, blood flow and effort are still supporting digestion instead of fully supporting football performance.

  • Heavy stomach = slower movement and poorer comfort.
  • Too much fat/fiber near kick-off can increase tummy upset risk.
  • Better plan: bigger meal the night before, then light and simple morning fuel.

Parent rule: Load fuel on Friday evening, not Saturday morning.

How to Cook + Ingredients (10 Options)

1) Chicken + Rice + Cooked Carrots

Ingredients: 2 cups cooked rice, 2 chicken breasts, 3 carrots, 1 tbsp olive oil, pinch of salt.

How to cook: Cook rice. Grill or pan-cook chicken with olive oil until fully cooked. Steam sliced carrots until soft. Serve warm.

2) Turkey Pasta + Light Tomato Sauce

Ingredients: 300 g pasta, 300 g turkey mince, 1 jar light tomato sauce, 1 tsp olive oil.

How to cook: Boil pasta. Brown turkey mince in olive oil. Stir in tomato sauce. Mix with pasta and serve.

3) Salmon + Potatoes + Peas

Ingredients: 2 salmon fillets, 6 potatoes, 2 cups peas, 1 tsp olive oil.

How to cook: Bake salmon at 200C for 14-16 mins. Boil potatoes and mash lightly. Steam peas and plate.

4) Lean Beef Mince Spaghetti

Ingredients: 300 g spaghetti, 300 g lean beef mince, 1 jar tomato sauce.

How to cook: Cook spaghetti. Brown mince and drain excess fat. Add tomato sauce and simmer. Serve over spaghetti.

5) Chicken Fajita Rice Bowl (Low Spice)

Ingredients: 2 cups cooked rice, 2 chicken breasts, 1 pepper, 1 onion, mild seasoning.

How to cook: Slice chicken and vegetables. Cook chicken first, add vegetables and mild seasoning. Serve over rice.

6) Tuna Pasta Bake (Light Cheese)

Ingredients: 300 g pasta, 2 tins tuna, 1 jar tomato sauce, 40 g light cheese.

How to cook: Cook pasta and mix with tuna + sauce. Place in oven dish, top lightly with cheese, bake 15 mins at 190C.

7) Jacket Potato + Beans + Grilled Chicken

Ingredients: 4 baking potatoes, 1 can baked beans, 2 chicken breasts.

How to cook: Bake potatoes at 200C for 50-60 mins. Grill chicken until cooked. Heat beans and serve together.

8) Stir-Fry Noodles + Tofu/Chicken

Ingredients: 2 packs noodles, 300 g chicken or tofu, 1 cup mixed vegetables, 1 tbsp low-salt soy sauce.

How to cook: Cook noodles. Stir-fry protein and vegetables, add noodles and soy sauce, toss for 2-3 mins.

9) Couscous + Chicken + Roasted Veg

Ingredients: 2 cups couscous, 2 chicken breasts, 1 courgette, 1 pepper, 1 tbsp olive oil.

How to cook: Roast chopped vegetables with olive oil at 200C for 20 mins. Grill chicken. Prepare couscous and combine.

10) Homemade Pizza (Thin Base, Lean Toppings)

Ingredients: 2 thin pizza bases, tomato passata, 120 g mozzarella, lean chicken/turkey slices, sweetcorn.

How to cook: Spread passata on base, add toppings lightly, bake at 220C for 10-12 mins.

Simple Parent Checklist (Match Morning)

  1. No new foods on match day.
  2. Keep breakfast light, mostly carbs, low fat.
  3. Use a small top-up snack close to kick-off.
  4. Bring water bottle and optional electrolyte drink.
  5. Pack recovery snack for straight after full-time.
