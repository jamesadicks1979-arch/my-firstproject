Simple Rule

Main meal should be around 3-4 hours before kick-off, then a light top-up snack 60-90 minutes before.

Do not overload food close to match time. A heavy stomach can reduce comfort and movement quality.

Timeline by Kick-Off

Kick-off Main meal (3-4h before) Top-up snack (60-90 mins before) Hydration
12:00 PM 8:00-9:00 AM: porridge + fruit + yogurt OR bagel + eggs (light) 10:30-11:00 AM: banana or toast + honey 500-700 ml across morning, then 150-250 ml pre warm-up
1:00 PM 9:00-10:00 AM: rice bowl + lean chicken/turkey 11:30-12:00 PM: fruit pouch or cereal bar 500-700 ml from breakfast to arrival, sip at warm-up
2:30 PM 10:30-11:30 AM: pasta + tomato sauce + lean protein 1:00-1:30 PM: rice cakes or banana 600-800 ml before match, extra electrolytes in heat

Best Main Meal Ideas (Lunchtime Kick-Off)

  • Pasta + tomato sauce + chicken/turkey
  • Rice + eggs + toast + fruit
  • Jacket potato + beans + tuna/chicken
  • Bagel + scrambled eggs + banana

Keep fat and very high-fiber foods lower in the final 2 hours pre-match.
